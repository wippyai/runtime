## Timer identity and reconciler join

Timers now have owned slots (strongTimer) with completion channels and once-only
completion. timerWork tracks scheduled and already-fired callbacks independently
of the name map. A callback only removes the exact slot it owns; stopping a timer
that has fired leaves its completion pending until the callback exits. stopTimers
seals new scheduling, cancels pending slots, and joins every retained callback.
Canceled joins are retryable and do not release ownership. Terminal-observation
cleanup stops timer slots outside strongState.mu to avoid recursive locking.

Reconciler lifecycle now owns cancellation, a completion channel and a worker
wait group. Watch exit cancels the owner, stops dissemination, joins timers and

restarting requires a fresh Service. StopReconciler(ctx) closes readiness,
